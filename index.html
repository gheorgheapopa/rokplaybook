Our Goal: Own a Province

What this alliance is working toward, what every member gets from it, and what we ask in return.

Our first big objective is simple: take and hold one full province in Zone 1. Every province is reached through several passes (gates), so owning one means holding all of its passes and the land between them. Everything in this playbook, from daily habits to alliance tech, builds toward that. Once the province is ours, we push into Zone 2 for the shrines, and later decide on the Lost Temple from a position of strength.

What comes with it

For every member
  • +25% gathering speed anywhere inside our territory, on main and farms.
  • Safe farming: with our passes held, enemies can't march in, and our territory blocks their teleports, so raids almost stop.
  • Holy site buffs for everyone: building and research speed (altars), commander XP and gathering (sanctums).
  • Alliance resource points inside our land, which pay for alliance tech.
  • A place to grow in peace before KvK, close to friends who help every timer.
For the alliance
  • A strong position in the kingdom: other alliances negotiate with us instead of attacking.
  • Easier recruiting: active players want to join an alliance that owns land.
  • A springboard to the Zone 2 shrines (Radiance: healing and AP, War: training) and, later, a say over the Lost Temple and the King's titles.
  • A united home base for the first KvK.

How we get there

WhenStep
Weeks 1–2Recruit a core of 15–20 active players, settle together in a Zone 1 corner, max member capacity and help techs.
Weeks 2–4Plant the Alliance Center and flags over the best tiles, research Flag Quantity, map every pass of the province and who sits behind it, and sign non-aggression pacts with those neighbours.
Weeks 4–8Take the passes one by one, starting with the one facing the strongest neighbour. Garrison them, hold one altar and one sanctum of each type, and cover the rest with flags.
Weeks 8–14Push into Zone 2 for Radiance and War shrines, prepare everyone for the first KvK.

The full details are in Chapter 07 (Alliance & Conquest): kingdom zones, taking a province, holy sites and alliance tech.

Our strategy for holding it

Fewer fronts
  • Pick a province with the fewest passes and the friendliest neighbours behind them.
  • Pacts with the neighbours behind the quiet passes, so we only ever defend one real front.
  • Settle our cities in the middle of the province, farms deepest inside, never next to a pass.
Every pass has an owner
  • Each held pass has a pass captain per timezone (EU / US / Asia) who keeps its garrison filled.
  • Anyone who sees enemies gathering near a pass posts it in #rally-calls. Everyone nearby shields up or reinforces.
  • If we can't hold every pass, we keep the core and the pass facing the biggest threat, and give up edges, never the centre.

The full plan, step by step, is in Chapter 07 under "Taking a province".

What we ask of you

Be active

Log in daily, help timers and donate to the starred alliance tech. Activity holds land, not raw power.

Live inside our land

Keep your main and farms inside alliance territory and gather there. Move with the alliance when we expand.

Show up for fights

Holy sites are contested every 3 days. Join rallies and garrisons when officers call, in your timezone.

Getting Started

Kingdom, accounts, civilization and the first hour. Mistakes here cost weeks later.

The kingdom

Brand-new kingdom

Start in a kingdom that has just opened (the server list marks new ones). Everyone starts at the same level, you get every new-kingdom event and its rewards, and the Hidden Lotus skin is still possible.

Plan the opening day

Get the core 10–15 players into Discord before the kingdom opens. Agree the alliance name, tag and a meeting point on the map, so everyone teleports to the same place in the first hour.

Pick the right corner

Settle in an empty corner of Zone 1 with good resource tiles, in a province with as few passes (gates) as possible and no big alliance next door. Never settle beside a big alliance's fort: you become their first target.

Three accounts

Every governor runs one main (the account you fight and grow with) and two farms (extra accounts that gather resources and send them to the main). Create all three on day 1, in the same kingdom, so the farms can ship resources from the very first hour.

Option A · PC (easiest)
  1. Install an Android emulator such as BlueStacks or LDPlayer.
  2. Use its multi-instance feature to open three copies of the game side by side: one for the main, one per farm.
  3. Log each instance into its own account. You can play all three at once.
  4. A PC with 8–16 GB of RAM handles three instances comfortably. Lower the graphics on the farm instances.
Option B · phone only
  1. Create the farms in the game itself: Settings → Account → Character Management lets you add new characters in the same kingdom and switch between them without logging out. The menu wording can differ slightly by game version.
  2. Each login, do the farms first (gather, transfer), then switch to the main last and spend what just arrived.
  3. An old phone or tablet makes a great second device: keep the farms logged in there and the main on your phone.
  4. On farms, turn off every notification except "under attack" and "shield expiring" so your phone stays quiet.

Name the accounts clearly, for example Name, Name F1, Name F2, so officers always know which farm belongs to whom.

The first hour

Do
  1. Finish the tutorial and the chapter quests (the quest list on the left of the screen). They give big piles of resources and speedups early on.
  2. Join our alliance, then use the free Beginner Teleport item to move your city next to the alliance. Do this on all three accounts.
  3. Redeem every gift code (Settings → Redeem) on all three accounts. Codes are posted in #codes.
  4. Send scouts to clear the fog. They find villages (free rewards) and caves (research, speedups).
  5. Queue a building and a research before every logout. An idle queue is lost growth.
  6. Turn on notifications for builder finished, shield expiring and being attacked.
  7. Keep the starter peace shield. Don't attack anyone in the first days. Fighting costs troops and slows growth.
  8. Spend action points on barbarians. The action point bar refills over time. When it's full, the extra is wasted.
Don't
  • Level many commanders at once. XP is limited. Spread over five commanders, none of them gets strong.
  • Use speedups before asking for help. Every alliance help cuts time off for free.
  • Spend gems on anything but VIP and the second builder. See Chapter 4 for the full gem order.
  • Open resource tokens (the resource items in your bag). Save them for events and emergencies.
  • Go inactive for hours. Every hour your builder sits idle is power you never get back.

Weeks are a typical F2P pace in a new kingdom. Active players with farms move faster.

Building priority

Buildings are your main source of power early on. Use one simple loop: upgrade City Hall → bring every other building up to the same level → upgrade City Hall again. City Hall can't go higher than the buildings it requires, so keeping everything level means you never get stuck waiting. The Wall is required for every City Hall level, so it's always part of the loop. Watchtower and Castle can wait until the late game, when the Tier 5 path needs them.

Order inside each loop
  1. City Hall: everything unlocks here
  2. Wall: required for every City Hall level
  3. The other building City Hall asks for (see the table above)
  4. Academy: faster research, and needed for troop tiers
  5. Barracks, Stable or Archery Range for your troop type
  6. Hospital: fewer troops die in fights
  7. Storehouse: protects resources from raids
  8. Trading Post: on all 3 accounts, it sets how much farms can send
  9. Alliance Center: more alliance helps per timer
  10. Farms, Lumber Mills, Quarries, Goldmine: production adds up

Commanders

Which commanders to build, how to level them, their talents, gear, formations and sculptures. This is where F2P accounts win or waste the most.

Commander rules

Commanders are where most F2P accounts waste resources. Follow these eight rules and every sculpture, tome and talent point counts.

Decide a primary for every army.

Each army has two commanders. The primary (first slot) brings their talent tree, equipment and armaments. The secondary only adds their skills. So talent points, gear and armaments only matter on primaries.

Example: in Bjorn + Sun Tzu, Bjorn wears the gear and uses his talents. Sun Tzu only needs his skills.

Keep secondaries light.

A secondary's talents do nothing, so don't pour XP tomes into them. Level them enough to work (around level 30) and spend their sculptures on skills.

Max the first skill to level 5 first.

Skill 1 is the active skill: it fires every time the rage bar fills and does most of the damage. Put sculptures into it until it's level 5 before touching skills 2–4.

Exceptions: Baibars wants skills 1 and 2 maxed. Gathering commanders want their gathering skill (Šárka skill 2, Constance skill 4).

Build Epics before Legendaries.

Epic sculptures are easy to get from civilization events, keys and shops, and a full Epic costs 440 sculptures. A fully built Epic (5-5-5-5) beats a Legendary stuck at 1-1-1-1. Finish 2–3 Epics in your first month, then start one Legendary.

Never stop a Legendary at 5 stars.

Stars raise a commander's level cap by 10 each. Guides advise stopping at 4 stars or pushing all the way to 6, because 5 costs a lot of XP for little gain on its own.

Universal sculptures only on long-term Legendaries.

Universal (gold) sculptures work on any Legendary and are very rare. Use them only on the 1–2 Legendaries you'll keep for months, like YSG or your chosen Prime.

Never on commanders you'll get from gold keys anyway (Charles Martel), never on Cleopatra, and never on a Wheel of Fortune commander the first time the Wheel runs.

One troop type for the main.

Gear sets, research and talents boost one troop type at a time. Split across infantry, cavalry and archers and you end up with three half-built armies. Pick one (infantry for the China path, cavalry if you started Spain) and build everything around it.

Get the talents right the first time.

Each commander has three talent trees, and resetting the points costs gems. Before you spend a point, check that commander's build below.

Taking a province

Owning a whole province is how our alliance becomes safe and rich. We take one in stages, in the right spot, at the right time: target weeks 4–8, after the core is recruited and member-capacity tech is done.

What we gain
  • Safe gathering: every member farms inside territory, with +25% gathering speed and almost no raids.
  • Every local holy site: the altars and sanctums inside are ours to hold.
  • Closed borders: with every pass held, enemies can't march in, and our territory blocks their teleports.
  • A home base: members grow in peace before KvK, and recruits want to join an alliance that owns an area.
What it costs
  • Defense: holy sites are contested every 3 days, so we need people online across timezones.
  • Flags: territory grows with flags, and our flag limit grows with alliance size and tech. Don't claim more than we can cover.
  • Attention: the top alliance may see a full province as a threat.
  • Spread: cities spread across a big area are harder to protect in a war than one tight cluster.
